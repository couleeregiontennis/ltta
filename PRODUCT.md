# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

1. **Tennis League Players**: Local recreational and competitive adult tennis players in the Coulee Region checking match schedules, court locations, team standings, individual rankings, finding subs on the Sub Board, and paying dues. Often viewing on mobile devices courtside or right after matches.
2. **Team Captains**: Managing rosters, entering match scores/results, coordinating lineups, and tracking team performance.
3. **League Administrators**: Generating seasonal schedules, managing players, tracking league dues/payments, auditing match edits, and broadcasting league announcements.
4. **General Public / Prospective Members**: Visiting the welcome/landing page to learn about the league, read rules, access resources, and join.

## Product Purpose

The official digital home and operations hub for the Coulee Region Tennis Association (CRTA / LTTA). It streamlines season scheduling, real-time standings, score reporting, player rankings, substitute coordination, and league administration in one accessible, high-performance web platform.

## Positioning

A community-first, dedicated tennis league platform tailored specifically to the regional division structure (e.g. Monday/Tuesday/Wednesday/Thursday leagues, court locations, local sub boards) with built-in instant score reporting and AI rules assistance ("Ask the Umpire").

## Operating Context

- Mobile courtside usage (checking next match, finding court directions, fast score entry under bright sunlight).
- Desktop captain and admin operations (scheduling generation, detailed standings review, roster management).
- Both light and dark mode preferences.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Stack**: React 18, Vite, React Router v6, Supabase (PostgreSQL & Auth).
- **Core Features**: Match Schedule, Standings, Player Rankings, Team Dashboards, My Schedule, Add Score, Captain Dashboard, Court Locations, Rules & "Ask the Umpire", Sub Board, Pay Dues, Admin Management (Schedule Generator, Player Management, Payment Management, Audit Logs).
- **Visual Goal**: Complete visual overhaul — modern, contemporary sports/league aesthetic with high craft, fluid mobile responsiveness, and crisp ergonomics.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Coulee Region Tennis Association (CRTA / LTTA).
- Sports heritage: Athletic, dynamic, high-contrast, energetic yet clear and professional.

## Product Principles

1. **Courtside Ergonomics First**: Essential data (who, when, where, score) must be glanceable and tappable with large, thumb-friendly touch targets on mobile.
2. **Bold, Contemporary Athletic Identity**: Clean modern typography, high contrast, crisp cards, vibrant accents that evoke modern tennis culture (Wimbledon greens, crisp clay accents, electric optic yellow/lime highlights).
3. **Clarity Over Noise**: Clean data tables, intuitive status badges, and streamlined flows for score entry and schedule lookups.
4. **Uncompromising Responsiveness**: Seamless experience from compact mobile viewports to wide desktop monitors.
